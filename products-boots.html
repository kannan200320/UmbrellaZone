Longevity Protocol

How To Care For Natural Rubber Footwear

Four simple rituals to keep your boots looking supple, clean, and 100% waterproof for years.

01

Rinse Off Mud & Road Grime

After stepping through muddy waterlogged streets, simply rinse boots under running tap water to keep rubber pores clean.

02

Wipe Clean With Soft Cloth

Wipe gently using a damp microfibre cloth. Natural rubber looks brand new after a quick 10-second wipe down.

03

Natural Air Drying

Never place rubber boots right in scorching sunlight or next to electric heaters. Let them air-dry naturally in a cool corner.

04

Upright Shoe Rack Storage

Store boots standing upright on your home shoe rack away from direct hot sun to keep the shape firm and flexible.
