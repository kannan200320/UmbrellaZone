Lifetime Care

Outerwear Longevity: How To Reactivate DWR

When water droplets stop beading on your jacket, follow this simple 4-step reactivation protocol to restore factory performance.

01

Wash with Tech Detergent

Never use standard household detergents or fabric softeners. Use additive-free technical wash like Nikwax Tech Wash.

02

Double Rinse Cycle

Run an extra cold rinse cycle to remove every trace of surfactant residue that might attract water molecules to the surface.

03

Tumble Dry on Gentle Heat

20 minutes of low tumble heat reactivates the fluorocarbon-free hydrophobic polymers, standing them upright like bristles.

04

Spray Re-Application

After 30 heavy rain days, lightly mist high-friction areas (shoulders, cuffs) with PFC-Free TX.Direct spray.
