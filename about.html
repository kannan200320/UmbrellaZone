Our Founding Mission

Engineered To Conquer The Heaviest Downpours

We were tired of seeing cheap, broken roadside umbrellas and leaking plastic ponchos thrown across crowded transit platforms after every heavy rain. We set out to engineer strong, reliable windproof umbrellas, 20,000mm waterproof raincoats, and vulcanized rubber rain boots built specifically for severe storms and the fiercest downpours.

The Testing Facility

Inside the Dynamic Wind & Storm Testing Lab

Located at our central testing campus, our specialized engineering workshop houses a customized aerodynamic wind testing rig. While roadside umbrellas break in the first 30 km/h breeze, every UmbrellaZone prototype is tested against continuous 80 km/h wind gusts and heavy cloudburst simulations.

We test rib hinge deflection, fluid drainage through our dual vortex canopy vents, and water repellency under severe horizontal storm rain. Only frames that survive 80 km/h storms without flipping inside-out are approved for urban and outdoor commuters.
