How To Care For Your Monsoon Gear

Proper handling preserves the hydrophobic lotus-leaf membrane and flexible frame hinges for decades.

01

Always Shake Dry

After stepping indoors, give the canopy 1-2 firm vertical shakes. The Teflon™ EcoElite nano-barrier will cast off 95% of moisture droplets immediately.

02

Air Dry Open

When convenient, leave the umbrella open for 15-20 minutes in a well-ventilated room before folding and zipping into its sleeve to prevent mildew.

03

Gentle Cleaning

For technical outerwear or boots, rinse with cold tap water. Do not use chemical fabric softeners as they coat the microporous waterproof membrane.
