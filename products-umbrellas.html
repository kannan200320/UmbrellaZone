Care & Longevity Protocol

The 10-Year Umbrella Maintenance Masterclass

Simple daily habits to keep your fluoropolymer canopy shedding water effortlessly and your mechanical springs responsive for a decade.

💨
Step 01 Hydrophobic Shake

The 5-Second Double Shake

Before entering your vehicle or office, snap the umbrella open and give two sharp downward shakes. The Lotus Nano-bead coating casts off 99% of surface droplets instantly, preventing moisture entrapment in the folds.

Drying Time: < 30 Seconds
🚿
Step 02 Coastal Care

Freshwater Salt De-Ionization

If walking along coastal shorelines where seawater spray strikes the canopy, rinse the fabric and rib joints with tap water once a month. This clears microscopic saline crystals and maintains silky glide movement.

Frequency: Monthly in Coastal Zones
☂️
Step 03 Open Storage

Post-Storm Vertical Relaxation

Never cram a soaked umbrella into a sealed handbag or glove compartment overnight. Prop it open tip-down in a well-ventilated corner to allow fiberglass rib tension to normalize and fabric weaves to breathe.

Lifespan Gain: +5 Years Service Life
