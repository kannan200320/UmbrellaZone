Corporate Procurement & FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for custom corporate branding?

Our minimum fleet size for custom 1-color logo silkscreening is 10 units. For bespoke Pantone canopy dyeing and custom laser handles, our minimum order is 50 units.

Do you provide GST invoices with Input Tax Credit (ITC)?

Yes, all corporate purchases include a 100% compliant GST invoice with your company GSTIN, enabling full input tax credit for your organization.

What payment methods do you accept for institutional orders?

We support UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Corporate Net Banking, NEFT/RTGS transfers, major credit cards, and Net-30 purchase order terms for approved enterprises.

How does the 2-Year Doorstep Monsoon Guarantee work for fleets?

Every corporate fleet is assigned a dedicated Online Commercial Service Desk. If any umbrella rib or joint suffers storm damage, simply send a photo on WhatsApp and we dispatch a free replacement directly to your office address.
