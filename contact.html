Help & Support

Frequently Asked Support Questions

Quick answers for customers before contacting our support desk.

Can I visit and test umbrellas in your wind rig before buying? +

Yes! Walk-ins are welcome Monday through Saturday at our Flagship Center between 10 AM and 8 PM. If you want a dedicated 30-minute demonstration with our materials engineer, please book a free appointment using the calendar form above.

How fast is doorstep delivery across regional zones? +

Orders placed before 2:00 PM are dispatched the same day. Express delivery takes 24 to 48 hours in metro zones. Other regions take 2 to 3 days, and remote areas take 3 to 4 days. Free delivery applies on all orders of ₹999 or more.

What if my umbrella rib breaks during heavy storms? +

Every UmbrellaZone product includes our 2-Year Doorstep Monsoon Guarantee. Simply send a photo of the issue to our support desk. Our team will verify it and dispatch a replacement unit to your doorstep with free reverse pickup.

What payment methods do you support? +

Yes, we accept all major digital wallets, debit/credit cards, net banking, and Pay on Delivery across our entire service network.

Can I trade in an old, broken umbrella of any brand for a discount? +

Yes! Through our Stormwear Recycling Program, you can drop off any broken umbrella at any of our regional experience centers to receive an instant credit voucher towards any new purchase.
